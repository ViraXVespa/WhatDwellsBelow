# Graphics actor

Status: current plan
Read when: billboard-alpha squash quads, player yard-mannequin foes, source-offset

Y-billboard frames stay nearest stickers. A sprite shader samples the light RT once at the actor's feet, with the ground shader's `light_origin` / `light_span` / `textureLod` tap, and multiplies that by the sticker and by `modulate`. Play does not call `LightRt.sample_xz` for actors. Actor stickers are created with `shaded` off (`BillSpr.bare` in `scripts/world/billboard_spr.gd`). The light they take is that multiply. Props and walls stay forward-lit. `App` runs `SpriteFilt.apply_sprite` on every new `Sprite3D`, and that filter only sets the texture filter. Do not turn `shaded` off there.

Floor mark is a near-black, low-alpha silhouette projected onto the floor. Player mark uses the current animation frame. Enemies use their still. The left sole pixel pins to the sticker's left foot and the right sole pixel pins to the right foot. Those sole pixels, and the opaque span the quad uses, are baked in `scripts/data/actor_soles.json` (`python tools/bake.py soles`). Play loads that table under the hub loader and looks the frame up. It does not scan sticker pixels for the sole. The contact row (`scripts/graphics/actor_lit/lit_contact.gd`) is one byte scan per texture, then a cache hit for that same texture. A missing file, a stamp mismatch, or a sprite that is not in the table is a hard error (`OS.crash`) that names the rebake. `bake.py check` fails when a sprite png, an fx sticker png, or the scan script changed since that bake. The table covers `assets/sprites` and `assets/fx` (the training dummy is `assets/fx/dummy.png`). The head edge shears away from the driving light. Width stays on the billboard axis. Stacked feet share one contact and keep the body width; they do not hinge the quad open. Length stays within about one body so a side stance does not become a needle. The quad must keep area when the light sits on the foot line: add a minimum camera-facing shear and smooth the warp so it does not pop. It must not read as a second actor.

Casters: the player and every enemy that uses the billboard helper (boss, named, summons). The training dummy and the hub vendor are not casters. Marks are a shared pool that starts at 6 nodes. A light volume checks a node out when the actor is inside and returns it on the way out. The hub sun counts as inside, so yard actors hold a sun mark. A stationary actor outside a volume holds none. The sun-sheared quad and the feet pin are cached when the frame is first seen (player warmup and `texture_changed`). Other lights re-aim that quad in the mark shader. Hub: one mark. Dungeon: up to three marks. Player UVs follow the current frame. Enemies stay on the still.

Hub squash follows the sun: one direction and one length for the whole yard. It does not rotate around a point on the dirt and does not grow as the actor walks. The hub crystal is a second mark on the player only, cast away from the crystal, not a copy of the sun quad. It is not the hub squash origin for the dummy. Dungeon squash uses up to three in-range torches, crystals, or campfires, one mark each. Nearest is strongest. Length follows that source's distance. Crystal marks stay long (ground lamp). Torches stay short when close (overhead). Alpha is split across those marks so they do not stack into a black smear. A mark hides when its alpha is under the hide floor. A source out of range drops its mark.

Mark sits on FLOOR_Y on floor cells only. Kill z-fight with depth bias, not a visible hover. Do not enable Sprite3D.cast_shadow. Do not parent the policy only under the player. Filter modes stay on the existing sprite filter owner.
