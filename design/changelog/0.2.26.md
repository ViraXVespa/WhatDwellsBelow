## 0.2.26
- Web touch overlay on phones and tablets
-- Dual stick: dynamic move, fixed aim
-- Hold-to-attack; double-tap latches until tap again
-- Hides when a keyboard or gamepad is present, and while menus are open
- Overlay uses existing pad glyphs and dungeon-themed wells

Summary: Phone browsers can now play Placeholdia and the dungeon with on-screen sticks and buttons when no physical keyboard or gamepad is connected.
