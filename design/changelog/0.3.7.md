## 0.3.7
- Anvil Analyze only accepts AT RISK green and blue pieces and destroys them in one confirm
- Analyze writes a forge book per type and rarity: item level, traits, and peak luck
- Forge is a configurator: type, rarity, item level, and optional trait locks
- Holds cap is three per type per slot; full cap keeps the old holds so you can replace or discard
- Whites stay starter-only; they do not appear on Analyze or Forge
- Duplicate extracts convert to smithing XP
- Forge spends gold and ore, plus wood on weapons and tools
- Gear can roll affixes (crit split, attack speed / range, health on hit / kill, gather traits)
- Anvil tabs highlight like Pause tabs; potion and food slots are disabled there

Summary: The Placeholdia anvil now analyzes AT RISK gear into a unlock book and forges new holds from that book instead of listing every remnant.
