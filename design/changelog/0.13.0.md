## 0.13.0

- New Xbox layout: X attacks, A does a hard attack, Y interacts, B goes back, RB dashes, and the D-pad uses a potion (right), eats food (left), opens the map (up) and toggles target lock (down). View opens your character and Menu pauses.
- New keyboard keys: P drinks a potion, F eats food and T toggles target lock. They stay on those letters on AZERTY, QWERTZ and Dvorak, and on Colemak, Food moves to G so it no longer shares Interact's key.
- Your custom bindings are kept; only controls you never changed move to the new defaults.
- The Controls page shows letter keys as just their keycap, so Map's M no longer looks like an H, and arrow keys read "Up" like the others.
- New Bow mode (Settings > Gameplay, off by default): hold LT or Shift to aim the longbow with the right stick or mouse, and fire with RT.
- Rebuilt phone controls: a floating move stick, big thumb buttons for Attack, Special, Dash and Use, slots for potion, food and target lock, and an aim stick in Bow mode, all kept clear of notches and the middle of the screen. Prompts in the world say Use while the touch controls are up.
- On phones, the pause journal is centred with every row visible, and on Android the Report Bug text fields now open the keyboard.
- Menus fade in and out instead of flashing onto the screen; turn it off under Settings > UI > Menu transitions.
- The camp HUD bar no longer covers the inventory, and the stat panel's iron card no longer has a checkered fringe around its border.
- Enemy "Lv" labels are larger on phones so they stay readable.
- The minimap and full map now mark nearby enemies, loot, chests, pots, Extraction Gates, shrines, campfires and the ghost shop, each with its own shape. Loot and objects drop off the map once taken or used.
- Extraction Gates always sit on a room's back wall instead of sometimes standing in the room or poking out of a side wall.
- Wall torches light up before you reach them, fit the wall at a smaller size, show their flame on side walls, and no longer flicker on wall corners.
- The web camp runs faster: lantern light skips the parts of the yard no lantern reaches, and the ground does less work per pixel, with no change to how it looks (camp idle 8.6 -> 12.4 fps and walking 11.0 -> 15.6 fps in a software-rendered test).
- The hub loading screen lifts as soon as frame times hold steady instead of waiting for 60 fps, so hub loads are about 3 seconds faster on slower devices.
- Load warmups were measured one by one. The yard, menu and player warmups stay because skipping them caused hitches of 0.1 to 1.3 seconds. GitHub Pages already compresses the web download (engine 39.5 MB -> 10.2 MB).
- Release web builds no longer bundle the local Godot AI editor addon (~1 MB), so a local build matches the live one.
- A slice can ask a local model to mark which functions to read first. When that pass misses, the session still starts from the usual seed.
- The OpenCode gather program is removed from slice startup.
- A local Comfy page can pose the player character from the character bible and try a style lock. Approve and deny notes, and good or bad marks on walk frames, stay with the project. In-game art is the same as last week.
- Dev: the screenshot tool captures menus and overlays again and finds the game scene after moving it into its capture view, so the text-size, map, crystal and shadow checks run again.
- Dev: web perf reports the loader's settle and warmup notes, and debug web builds can read live game values for profiling.
- Dev: nightly self-tests pass again, the docs tool no longer rejects a unique tunable key, and the Settings UI page moved into its own script.

Summary: Week 13 brings a new Xbox and keyboard layout, Bow mode, rebuilt phone controls, menu fades, map markers for enemies and loot, better torches and gates, and a faster web camp.
