## 0.2.22

- On-screen prompts follow last-used input. One scheme at a time: pad glyphs after a gamepad event, keyboard and mouse glyphs after a key or click.
- Menus draw Select / Back (and extra actions) in a footer strip at the bottom-right of the panel. Button captions are verbs only.
- Tab headers show LB / RB (or `[` / `]`). The row stretches across the menu and scrolls when tabs overflow.
- Gear-board stats paging stays on the card: Q / E on keyboard, LT / RT on pad.
- World interact prompts are a verb plus the current `interact` glyph on the HUD.
- Glyphs are chunky pixel assets under `assets/ui/prompts/`. Rebuild with `python tools/gen_prompt_glyphs.py`.

Summary: Prompts swap with the last device you touched and use real key / mouse / pad art instead of baked A / ENTER text.
