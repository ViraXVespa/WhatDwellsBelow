## 0.5.34

- Web build load and play speed (no look change): the web build now reads the baked `hub_light` atlas like desktop instead of repainting the hub light at load. Look is the same as the desktop bake; if the baked copy cannot be read it falls back to the old runtime path.
- Dungeon music now plays as a stream (not a sample) so it is less likely to drop out, and the track is loaded first when the hub loads, ahead of the other assets. Needs a real-device listen on web.
- Warm-up frames (`pump_fps`) now stop early once the frame rate is steady and are capped at 6 frames (was 12).
- New dynamic render scale: the 3D view stays at full resolution (scale 1.0) and only drops in 0.1 steps, to a floor of 0.6, when the frame rate stays under 40 fps for about 1.5 s. It climbs back after about 12 s at 55+ fps. Tunables `DYNRES_*` in `tunables.gd`; off with `DYNRES_ON` false or the launch flag `--wdb-no-dynres` (web `?wdb-no-dynres`).

Summary: Web performance work: hub lighting loads from the baked atlas, dungeon music streams, warm-up ends sooner, and the 3D view can lower its resolution on slow machines. At normal frame rates nothing looks different.
