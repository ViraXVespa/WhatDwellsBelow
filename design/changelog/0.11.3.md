- The `wdb` MCP answers in fewer calls: `all=true` returns a whole lookup or check in one reply, `lookup reads=[...]` answers several reads at once, pages are about 60 lines, `names` lists every function, and a doc without a heading returns its outline with line numbers.
- `slice.py open` gains `--seed-only`, `--in WT` (an existing worktree) and `--launch WT --review`; the gather agent now sees the User's request, is told to batch its reads, and stops only at a 30-round runaway cap.

- Fixes from the second gather run: `adjust demote` and `set-condition` take their condition as text or `--when`, `adjust --help` lists every op, a failing op's error always prints in full (also with `--dry-run`, and in `--json`), `--verbose` exists, `find.py xref --path` accumulates repeated paths, `wdb_mcp.py call --help` lists each tool's arguments, a doc without a heading stays its outline even with `all=true`, and identical batched reads run once.
- Report Bug on phones and tablets: tapping a text field now opens the on-screen keyboard in the web build, the Door and Job lists take two taps (the first picks a row and shows its description, the second closes the list) and scroll instead of covering the description, Email me about this report stays off and disabled until you type a valid email and then checks itself, and the privacy line now promises your data is never sold and is used only for debugging and messages you authorize.
- The start card's ref and merge-back follow the checked-out branch; `check.py load-graph` no longer flags the slice's `session-context.md`.

Summary: The Report Bug page works on touch screens (keyboard, two-tap lists, email opt-in, privacy promise); behind the scenes, the slice gather and the repo MCP waste fewer turns and scope context more tightly.
