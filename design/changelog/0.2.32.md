## 0.2.32
- Hits use opaque sprite occupancy against the telegraph, not a cylinder at the target origin
-- Transparent pixels do not register
-- Axe / staff: full damage in the center and start of the fan; falloff only at the far tip
-- Crits only on planted coverage
- Longbow idle aim is one yellow shoulder-height line; no second tele and no red flash
-- Arrows face travel, sit on that line, and test the head as they move
-- Planted hits stop the arrow; a glance may pierce one more body at reduced damage
-- Breakables never eat arrow damage
-- Special is yellow spread lines, not a filled cone
- Placeholdia dummy uses the same occupancy test, ignores knockback, and refills at 0 HP
-- Dark pad under the dummy; Welcome banner is double size
- Floating damage numbers hold, then fade; glance numbers are smaller and dimmer
-- Number and outline sort in front of the target and its health bar

Summary: Combat reads what you see on the sprite. Melee tapers at the fan tip, the bow is a real projectile on a yellow path, and Placeholdia has a dummy to test it.
