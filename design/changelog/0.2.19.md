## 0.2.19
- Weapon and tool stay equipped in Placeholdia and on the next run
-- Empty slots after a new save, load, death, or Dispel refill the selected hold when one is picked, otherwise the current starter
-- Armor, potion, and food can still be empty

Summary: You no longer load into the hub or a dungeon with empty hands. The kit on the avatar is the hold you last picked, or the starter Great Axe and pickaxe (or hatchet) if you have not picked a hold.
