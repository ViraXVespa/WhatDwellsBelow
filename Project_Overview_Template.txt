Feedback convention (demo vs full game)
The player does not label notes as demo vs full game. Grok decides:
- If it belongs in the **public playable demo** — the included dungeon loop (Great Axe + pickaxe, 2–6 floors, hold-attack / dash / slam, mine, clerks, smashables, death/dispel recap, pad UI) **or** the look/feel/honesty of those systems so a stranger would take the game seriously — **build it**.
- **Do not expand the demo city** (Placeholdia) unless the player explicitly asks. It is a staging fragment: crystal, guild, vendor, anvil, dumpster. City notes go in this file.
- If it is a full-game system that this demo simply **omits** (other skills, biomes, extra weapons, co-op camera, stealth, story, Combat Level UI, the real city), **write it here**. Do not stub it as a greybox; leave it out until it can ship at demo quality.
- This build is a **promotional playable demo / technical proof of concept**, not an in-studio vertical slice. It should feel like the finished game with features cut, not a limited prototype. Polish of included systems is in-scope. Hub expansion is not.
- When unsure, park it in this file and ask only if the demo would be built wrong without an answer.

1. Pitch & pillars
================================================================

One-liner
Decided (public): **A gamepad-first dungeon crawler where you pilot disposable spirit avatars, mail loot home, and slowly remember the skills you earned in the dark.**

Elevator pitch (from original)
WDB is a mashup of RuneScape and Heroes of Hammerwatch. Gameplay is gamepad-focused with a 2D art style closer to Hammerwatch than RuneScape. The player empowers an extravagant number of skills and abilities through play. Skills such as woodcutting, mining, and smithing gather resources and craft armaments. Weapons each have their own experience and levels: big bonuses for that weapon type, smaller bonuses for all weapons, to encourage using a variety of weapons.

Play spaces
- City (**Vylenheim**, provisional): static hub with vendors and skilling facilities. Layout only changes via story progress or facility upgrades. Contains the dungeon entrance.
- Dungeon: randomly generated each delve. Collect materials, gain XP, reach lower floors. No return to the city once inside.

Core fantasy
You do not physically enter the dungeon. You transfer consciousness into a Spirit Avatar. Avatars cannot leave; the connection must be destroyed or dispelled to wake up. Death/recall feels like waking from a dream: most of the adventure is forgotten, a fragment remains.

Pillars
Decided (public pitch — all five). Demo is solo; co-op is still a pillar of the *game*, sold as coming:
1. Every delve feels like a new adventure; permanence is a fragment, not a full carry-over.
2. Extraction is the real win condition — send it up or lose it.
3. Gamepad-first Diablo/Hammerwatch action: smaller enemy groups, longer fights, not trash-mob swarms.
4. Skills and weapon mastery are the long-term character. No classes; same starting stats; you are what you play. Equipment is visible on the 64×64 sprite.
5. Solo and co-op are both first-class. Modes get unique tweaks, not just a player-count slider.

Non-goals
Decided:
- Not an MMO / not a persistent shared world. Co-op is party-based (form up in the city, enter together), not drop-in lobby-as-hub.
- Not click-to-move RuneScape combat.
- Not Hammerwatch-style high-quantity trash-mob waves; fights are smaller groups and last longer.
- Not full permadeath of the player character / account.
- Not grimdark; dying is a shitty work hazard, not cosmic horror.
- Not a dating sim by default (DanMachi is a *tone* reference, not a genre reference) unless we add that later.

Tone
Decided: **hopeful and humorous**. Universe tonality in the neighborhood of *Is It Wrong to Try to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?* — dungeon diving is a **noble career path**, not a cursed last resort. Wonder at what could be found below.
Decided: death is an **inconvenient inevitability** of the job. Player-side it can sting from *frustration* (almost had the next floor). In-universe it **hurts like hell**; that shock is a big reason adventurers lose most memories of a delve (the XP-fragment rule has a diegetic cause).
Decided: not grimdark. Recap screen can be light; the lore text can admit it hurt.

Comparable games (steal vs reject)
- Heroes of Hammerwatch: steal 2D gamepad dungeon crawling, right-stick aim, melee-forward facing, town/dungeon split, passive dodge-as-stat, party of independent characters entering together. Reject high-quantity mob swarms and very short trash fights.
Decided: **no shop-between-floors.** Hidden **in-dungeon shops** (HoW-style finds) spend **unextracted gold**. **In the demo too.**
Decided: **permanent town upgrades = Vylenheim only.** Placeholdia never upgrades.
- Diablo: steal smaller packs, longer combat, passive block, mix of automatic defense and a few bound active skills. Reject isometric click-to-move and PC-first ARPG inventory-as-gameplay (unless later decided).
- Heroes of Hammerwatch 2 / Halls of Torment: steal the 3/4 overhead camera (square tiles, readable on a TV). Art follows HoW2 (pixel sprites), not Halls of Torment's retro-3D rotating models.
- RuneScape: steal skills, gathering, crafting, weapon diversity. **Reject** tick combat, click-to-move, and a **Grand Exchange**. **Light quest journal later** (optional guild notes, not RS quest-req checklists). Not in the demo.

2. Player fantasy & identity
================================================================

Who is the player on the surface?
Decided: create-a-character. Each player (including each couch player) makes their own adventurer. Not a single named hero you merely dress.
Decided: adventuring is a noble career. The player is (or is becoming) a professional dungeon diver — origin can be **dumpster-diving behind the guild** until the receptionist recruits them (optional intro).

Who is the avatar in the dungeon?
Decided: a Spirit Avatar piloted by transferring consciousness. Bound by dungeon magic; cannot exit intact.
Decided: in co-op, each player has their own avatar (not one shared body).

Solo vs co-op
Decided: both modes are a key part of the game. It must work well solo and in co-op, with extra tweaks so the modes feel different from each other.
Decided: co-op players form a party in the city, then all enter the dungeon at the same time.
Decided (mode-tweak example): each player is limited to a single resource-gathering tool per delve. Solo, mixed chokepoints are impassable (trees + rocks if you only brought a pickaxe; vines if you didn't bring a sickle). Co-op, a balanced tool split (hatchet + pickaxe + sickle) can open those paths.
Decided: max party size is 4.
Decided: couch co-op on console is the ideal vision (friends on one TV). Solo console is second. Online is not the orientation.
Decided: **online later, don’t block it, not in the slice.** Couch/local is v1. Keep seedable dungeons and per-player banks so online can reuse the same rules. Online players would each have a full screen (no two-pane).
Decided: any co-op player may leave at any time (dispel or death). They **cannot rejoin that dungeon**. Remaining party continues. On couch co-op, that player's avatar is gone — same as if they died (no extra linger/spectate rule unless we add one later).
Decided: per-player. Your extracts go to **your** bank and **your** gear analysis. Co-op is not a shared mailbox. **No trading** (gear is progression; sharing would power-level).
Decided: floor *access* in co-op uses the party's best (any member's deepest). Personal banks/XP stay per-player.

Classes vs skills
Decided: no classes. One character empowered by many skills/abilities. All players start with the same stats and become different through gameplay (RuneScape-style).
Decided: character creation is appearance (HoW2-like options) plus a starting **weapon** and starting **gathering tool** — not a class pick.
Decided: avatar appearance is customizable and must read in-world at 64×64 (not just on a paper-doll menu).
Decided: anything equippable is visible on the sprite (armor, weapon, tool, etc.) **in the full game**.
Decided: appearance sliders match **HoW2-like depth** (hair, skin, body, clothing colors) when customization ships. **Not in the demo.**
Decided (**full game, not demo**): **achievements** (e.g. "Heal 100K from food") unlock **permanent buffs** and **titles**. Titles grant **Artifact-like bonuses from the start of a run**. None of this is in the demo.
Decided (**demo exception**): **armor still drops** (white/green) and works as gear, but **no doll / no sprite layers**. Stats apply; you look like the baked starter until customization.

What "stronger" feels like
- After one good **demo** run: mailed **ore/gold** *and* a **visible power bump** from persistent XP (axe hits harder, mining channels faster). Recap sells both.
- After 10 hours: a **real kit + skill identity** — forged rolls you care about, noticeable skill levels, **Vylenheim starting to build up** (Placeholdia is demo-only and stays a fragment).
- After 100 hours: **deep floors, many skills, living city** — extravagant skills, upgraded Vylenheim, weapon masteries, endless after the campaign beat.

4. Core loops
================================================================

Minute-to-minute
Decided combat verbs: move, aim (or target-lock), **hold to attack** in that direction, **dash**, let passive dodge/block work, fire the occasional bound active.
Decided gathering: RuneScape-style channel (walk up, interact, stand while a bar fills). Full verbs: move, fight, channel-gather, use floor facilities, greed vs extract.
Decided extract: **dialogue with an NPC** at an extraction station (not a silent shrine channel). "What to send" picker, then send. Stations are **non-combat areas** — you get a real decision, not a panic channel under aggro.

One delve
Decided flow:
1. In the city, choose to enter the dungeon.
2. Configure loadout.
3. Enter; cannot return to the city.
4. Push floors: kill, gather, find extract facilities, find stairs.
5. Town: big entrance crystal to any floor you've ever reached. In-dungeon: crystals skip **down** to previously reached deeper floors. Stairs are down-only.
6. Extract resources/gear to the surface when you find a facility.
7. Terminate the avatar (death or pause-menu dispel) to wake up.
8. Keep extracted unlocks + a small fragment of XP; lose unsent resources and most XP.

Decided: voluntary wake is **Pause Menu → Dispel Avatar (Return to Town)** (Hades / Hammerwatch). Not a world object. It is **abandoning the run** — the only way to send items home is extract facilities. Dispel is going home empty-handed on whatever you didn't extract.
Decided: a good run is **20–30 minutes** (several floors, a couple of clerk stops, then death/dispel recap). Shorter “one floor and mail” and longer greed pushes are allowed, not the balance target.
Decided: success is what you **extracted** (plus the XP fragment), not whether you lived. Dying and pause-dispel are the same kind of exit; a death after mailing gear home is still a good run. A dispel with a full unsent bag is abandoning.

Between delves (city meta)
Decided: vendors, anvils, public kitchen, facility upgrades, story-driven layout changes, loadout before next delve.
[TODO] Other hub verbs: bank, skill training dummies, quest NPCs, recreation forge, museum of extracted gear?

Win / lose / exit rules
- Unsent resources and **unforged bag gear**: lost on death/termination. (decided)
- XP: only a small fragment is permanent. (decided)
- **Forged** holds **snap back** on death (even if dropped). **Sell/destroy** frees a hold; recipe stays. Recipes are unlimited; **3 usable forged items per type**.
Decided: extracted raw materials persist as **banked stacks** on the surface (50 ore sent = 50 ore in your bank). Gear uses the stash → analyze → forge loop in §9.
Decided: extract does **not** end the run. You send stuff up at a facility, then keep delving or later abandon via pause dispel / death. Extract = mail home; dispel = wake up.

5. Combat
================================================================

Model
Decided: melee-forward Hammerwatch, mixed with Diablo. Real-time action, not click-to-move.
Decided: smaller enemy groups and longer fights than Hammerwatch. Not a high-quantity mob-swarm game.
Decided: right stick aims. R3 toggles target mode — focus one enemy; right stick changes target while locked.
Decided: dodge and block are passive skills (Hammerwatch dodge / Diablo block). Automatic. Optional bound actives such as Shield Raise exist on top, not instead.
Decided: **Dodge** = % chance the hit **fully evades** (no damage). **Block** = % chance to **reduce** the hit (not a full immune).
Decided: **Dash** is in the **slice**. Hammerwatch-like **i-frames**, on **cooldown** (no stamina). Separate from passive Dodge.
Decided: starting **Sword** is 1-handed; it **comes with a starter shield** to offset that (the protection is the shield, not the sword type). Shield stays its own armor slot — you can replace the starter. Starting **Wand** likewise **comes with a tome** to offset 1-handed.
Decided: hold-to-attack (trigger/button). You keep swinging/shooting while held; release to stop. Fits longer Diablo-like fights and leaves interact free for RS-style gathering channels.
Resolved: Dodge = full evade chance; Block = DR chance; Dash has Hammerwatch i-frames.
Decided: **mana bar** for some skills. Other skills are **cooldown-only** (no mana). Hold-to-attack basic swings do not spend mana. **No stamina bar** — dash is cooldown + i-frames.

Weapons in play, not just stats
Decided: many weapon types, per-type XP, type bonus + smaller global bonus. These nine are **starting weapons only**; many other types exist later.
Starting weapons (creation pick — one of):
- Great Axe — longer-range melee arc, slower.
- Sword — 1-handed, mid-range melee arc, moderate speed. Starts bundled with a **shield** (offset for 1H). Weapon type is **Sword**, not "shield-and-sword."
- Dual Daggers — fast; a quick burst of two short-range melee arcs.
- Longbow — long-range single-target projectile, slower.
- Shortbow — mid-range single-target projectile, moderate speed.
- Crossbow — shorter-range single-target projectile, fast.
- Greatstaff (Fire) — long-range magic, large AoE projectile, slower.
- Staff (Lightning) — mid-range magic, chain projectile, moderate speed.
- Wand (Arcane) — 1-handed, short-range magic, single-target projectile, fast. Starts bundled with a **tome** (offset for 1H), same idea as Sword+shield.
[TODO] Full post-start weapon-type list (spears, hammers, thrown, etc.).
Decided (partial): combat has both **character traits** (Strength, Defense, Dexterity, …) and **per-type weapon mastery**. Magic has **Magic Masteries** in addition to staff/wand types.
Decided: swinging a weapon trains **that weapon's mastery** plus a **little of the matching trait** (Great Axe → Great Axe + Strength). Magic weapons → type + Magic Masteries. Demo HUD can still only show Great Axe / Mining / Smithing.
Decided: Dual Daggers = **two hits per attack tick** while hold-to-attack is held (fast double-hit, not a combo sequence).

Damage / roles
Decided (at least): physical melee, physical ranged, and elemental magic (Fire / Lightning / Arcane on the starting caster weapons).
[TODO] More damage types later? How armor/resist interacts. Starter Sword's protection comes from the bundled **shield** (block chance), not from the sword type itself.
Decided (**demo**): **Slam staggers ~0.5s** (readable punish, not a stun-lock). Guardian stagger is shorter. Burn / slow / other stuns wait.
Decided (**demo fire traps**): hit = **~40 HP** of 100, you keep going. Cycle **~2s safe / 1s flame**. Dash **i-frames can carry you through**. Puzzle-only rooms (no packs). Optional chest-path only; never on stairs.
Decided (**demo plates/levers**): levers stay flipped. **Solo: plates latch open.** **Co-op: stand-to-hold** so one player can open the chest; **once the chest is opened the plate latches** so the standing player can still receive their loot if loot-mode would grant them any. Never on stairs.
Decided slice: **three roles**, smaller packs, longer fights: **Melee bruiser**, **Ranged**, **Tank**. No boss. Extra roles later.
Decided: **story bosses at campaign beats** in the full game. **Slice: a basic Floor Guardian every 3rd floor** (3 and 6). Stairs stay locked while it lives. Slice caps at floor 6.
Decided slice melee: bruisers and tanks **telegraph a lunge** (windup pose, then dash-strike). Direction locks at windup so the player can sidestep. Contact-walk-into-you is not the melee kit.
Decided slice ranged: goblin archer **draws** (windup sprite) then looses. Shot direction locks on the draw.
Decided: enemies need **line of sight**. Walls block aggro, shots, and melee windup. They may hunt a last-seen point briefly after losing LOS, then idle. Player axe/slam also cannot hit through walls.
Decided: **enemy health bars** (world-space) so you can read how close a pack is to dropping. Floor Guardian keeps the HUD bar too.

Gathering during combat
Decided (for now): interruptible, but allowed — you can start a channel with enemies around; taking a hit (or moving?) interrupts the channel. If keeping combat keybinds during a channel is awkward on a gamepad, we may swap to "clear the room first." Revisit after the first playable.
Decided: a gather channel is interrupted by **taking damage** or **moving**. Attacking isn't separately needed (you'd have to move or cancel interact). Revisit if pad-feel is bad.

Death in the dungeon
Decided: avatar destroyed/dispelled; consciousness returns; dream-like memory loss.
Decided: pause-menu Dispel Avatar is the voluntary version of the same exit. Same rewards/losses as dying for unsent stuff (extract is the only send-home). Co-op: leaver is out of this run; others continue.
Decided recap (death and pause-dispel): **light tone**. Show floor reached, XP fragment kept per skill, materials banked, gear analyzed (or that there was no Gopher), gold mailed vs lost, bag that vanished. Co-op: **party recap**, each player a **Gauntlet-style strip** of their own results.

6. Dungeon laws
================================================================

Generation
Decided: layout random each entry; per-floor enemies, resources, and aesthetics stay consistent between delves. Aesthetics are **biome-driven** (dungeon / field / cave / sky city / …).
Decided: **mixed by biome**, not one topology for the whole hole. A floor's biome picks the vibe and the layout language. Examples: traditional dungeon, open fields, caves, even a **sky city**. "The dungeon" is a stacked otherworld, not only stone corridors.
Decided slice biome: **traditional dungeon** (rooms/corridors, stone). Campaign biome list later.
Decided: **same floor laws in every biome, different dressing.** Crystal / down-exit / clerks / bag / extract / gold / tool-gates-as-side-paths always apply. A sky-city “stairs” can be a balloon; an open field can be a cave mouth. Biomes may add **extra flavor traits** (weather, mounts, updrafts, etc.) on top — they don’t replace the laws. [TODO] List flavor traits per biome later.
Decided: floors are **expansive** — branching rooms, long dead-end corridors, hidden loot stashes at the far end of those dead ends (gold / ore / white-green gear). Stairs can be found before you've looted; side paths are optional RNG dopamine. A full clear can overshoot the 20–30 min "good run"; skipping to stairs is valid.
Decided: generation must be shareable across a party (same dungeon at the same time). Treat layouts as seedable.
Decided: **stairs are always reachable with any one tool (or none).** Tool gates are **side paths, extra nodes, extra clerks, extra loot** — never the only way down. Co-op sees more of the floor; solo still completes the loop. Trees / rocks / vines / etc. block bonuses, not the campaign path.

Vertical structure
Decided: stairs to the next floor (down only — no walking back up). Crystal at the entrance of each floor.
Decided crystal rules:
Decided: in-dungeon crystal is **next to the floor spawn / entrance**, not rolled into a random room. You appear beside it.
- **From town:** a special, bigger crystal in the city's dungeon entrance. Teleport to **any floor you have reached in any run**.
- **From inside the dungeon:** crystal skip to **any floor deeper than your current floor** that you reached on a **previous** run. This-run new depth is walked via stairs first. No crystal-up.
- **Co-op:** the party may access up to the **deepest floor any member has reached** (their personal-best is shared for access).
Decided: **finite campaign path, then endless after**. Beat the campaign beat / "the thing," then deeper challenge floors keep going.
Decided: campaign path is **100 floors**, then **endless**. Demo still caps at 6 and loops.
Decided demo: **floors 1–6**, looping. A good run is still 20–30 min; shorter and greedier runs are allowed.

Contents per floor
Decided: enemies, resource nodes, random facilities that can send resources/gear to the surface.
Decided extract clerks:

Gathering clerks — **1 per floor**, type from:
- Mining → Pickaxe → Ore → **Miner**
- Woodcutting → Hatchet → Wood → **Lumberjack**
- Harvesting → Sickle → Plants → **Alchemist**
- Quarrying → Stonehammer → Stone → **Stonemason**
- Fishing → Tacklebox → Fish → **Fishmonger**

Misc clerks — **1 per floor**, type from:
- Gear → **Gear Gopher**
- Misc → **Guild Runner**

Special clerks — **random**:
- All → **Packmule Patty**

So a floor always has 1 gathering clerk + 1 misc clerk; Patty is extra luck. Confirmed: misc is **1-from-2** — a floor can have a Guild Runner and **no Gear Gopher**. Gathering clerk may not match your tool. Gear mail is not guaranteed every floor.
Decided: clerks are **hidden in safe alcoves / side rooms**, not parked at the entrance crystal. Stairs can be found before extract — hunting the station is the tension. Entrance is crystal + spawn only.
Decided: **any** of those clerks can instead mail **gold** (50% of carried → town, 10% clerk fee, 40% stays on you). That **clerk instance** is then spent — no ore/gear from them after.
Decided slice: Hammerwatch-style **breakable pots/barrels** scatter in combat rooms. They smash on a hit and can drop **gold** plus **HP pickups** (walk-over orbs). **No mana orbs** while mana is hidden. Microtasks, not a skill. Full-game roster can add more smashables.

Decided: floors are combat + gathering nodes + stairs/crystal + extract clerks, plus **fun random finds**. First-pass extra roster (all of these exist):
- Production stations (anvil, etc.)
- Campfires
- Shrines
- Ghost vendor (in-dungeon shop)
- Rest spots
- Puzzle bits
[TODO] Full-game extras: rest spots, production stations, heavier puzzle sets — spec per type later; they should feel like discoveries, not homework.
Decided (**demo finds**): **campfire** (heal **HP**, **one sit** — no mana until something spends it), **shrine** (**+20% damage for 45s**, timer on HUD), **hidden ghost shop** (safe room like clerks), **Artifact chests**, light **movement puzzles** as chest hiding spots: **timed fire-breathing traps** (puzzle-only, no packs), **cracked walls** (any axe hit including Slam), **plates and levers** opening **gates/doors**. These never block stairs. Rest spots / extra production stations stay full-game.
Decided (**demo shop**): **~1 in 3 floors**, parked in **dead-end loot rooms** (optional corridors, not clerk alcoves, not the entrance). Spend **unextracted gold**. **New ghost/shopkeep sprite** — do not reuse the Placeholdia stall vendor. Voice: **hopeful banter, a bit eerie**.
Decided: ghost shop **cannot mail gold** (or anything else) to town. Extract clerks only. The shop room is **non-combat / safe**, like a clerk alcove.
Decided: shop lists **2–4 Artifacts** (player may **buy at most 2** from that shop) plus a **small food-only snack selection** (**25g**, vs stall **5g**). After 2 Artifact buys, leftovers stay **listed but locked**; snacks remain. Leaving does **not** reset the 2-buy cap. Player **can pawn gear or potions** for **very little gold** (bag space, or a few coins short). **Must bag the equipped potion before pawning** — shop buys from the bag, not the HUD slot. 14. Playable demo vs full vision
================================================================

Purpose of this build
This version is a **public playable demo** and **technical proof of concept**, meant to be shared (Patreon and similar) so people can feel the game and want to back the rest. It is not an internal vertical slice, greybox, or “fun with rectangles” studio prototype.

Quality bar
- **Looks and feels like the final game.** Included rooms, combat, HUD, and art should be presentable to a stranger on a couch.
- **Features are omitted, not stubbed.** If a system is not in the demo, it is absent — not a rectangle, not a “coming soon” dummy, not a broken half-feature.
- **Honesty of what *is* in.** LOS, bars, extract, recap, pad UI, and the plaza→dungeon→wake loop have to play as themselves.

Full vision (from original)
Extravagant skills, many weapon types, a living city, deep procgen dungeon, extraction/recreation, dreamlike XP, story that can reshape the hub. Couch co-op on Xbox is the long-term design center.
