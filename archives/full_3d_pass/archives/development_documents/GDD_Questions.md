### **1.2 The Five Pillars – Which Are Actively Demonstrated vs. Merely Promised**

**Current data** (verbatim from Template §1)

1. Every delve feels like a new adventure; permanence is a fragment, not a full carry-over.  
2. Extraction is the real win condition — send it up or lose it.  
3. Gamepad-first Diablo/Hammerwatch action: smaller enemy groups, longer fights, not trash-mob swarms.  
4. Skills and weapon mastery are the long-term character. No classes; same starting stats; you are what you play. Equipment is visible on the 64×64 sprite.  
5. Solo and co-op are both first-class. Modes get unique tweaks, not just a player-count slider.

**Questions that still need locking**

  * All should be clear.

  * The equipment being visible is a full game feature. The demo should still use a realtime composite (paper doll style) player model like the final game, but it should be locked to art of one specific loadout.   
  * Pillar 5 is currently solo-only.   
* For pillar 4: the demo currently shows only Great Axe \+ Mining \+ a few secondary skills and does **not** show equipment layers on the sprite. Is the “Equipment is visible on the 64×64 sprite” clause waived for the demo, or must it be implemented even in the vertical slice?  
  * See previous response.

* `SaveData.load_or_create()`: if the JSON is missing or fails to parse → create a fresh save, seed starters, restock, and write.  
* Invalid `presentation` values are forced back to `"live"`.  
* Missing textures/streams fall back silently (e.g., music, portraits).  
* `wipe_save` deletes the file and reloads a clean save.  
* Template: browser `user://`; new machine/browser \= new diver; no other recovery rules stated.

**Questions that still need locking**

* Must a corrupted or partially readable save always result in a completely fresh diver, or should partial recovery of XP/gold/holds be attempted?  
* What exact fallback assets (default sprite, silent SFX, placeholder music) are required when files are missing?  
* Should the GDD mandate any user-facing warning when a save is reset or assets fail to load?  
* Is there a maximum save-file size or validation step that must be enforced?

### **16.6 Exact Behavior When Stairs Are Locked on the Final Floor**

**Current data**

* `SLICE_MAX_FLOOR := 6`; stairs are spawned with a locked flag when `current_floor >= SLICE_MAX_FLOOR`.  
* On boss floors (3 and 6\) stairs remain locked while the Floor Guardian lives.  
* No explicit player-facing message or alternative interaction is coded beyond the locked flag.  
* Template: “Stairs stay locked while it lives. Slice caps at floor 6.” Main path is never tool-gated.

**Questions that still need locking**

* What exact visual, audio, and interaction feedback must appear when the player approaches locked stairs on floor 6 (or a living Guardian)?  
* Must the locked state be permanent on floor 6 even after the Guardian is dead, or does killing the Guardian unlock a “demo complete” exit?  
* Should any special recap or achievement trigger when the player reaches the locked final stairs?  
* Is there an alternative way to end the run from floor 6 (crystal, forced dispel, etc.) that must be documented?
