## 0.5.0

- Opened development week 5 on the live path
- Placeholdia yard uses seamless grass and packed-dirt sheets; the path stays cobble-tinted; a post-and-rail fence rings the slab
- Guild and reception sit as 3D boxes with russet shingles and a short striped awning; the vendor stall keeps its canvas tarp
- Dummy pad, floor crystal, anvil, and notice board keep labeled spots; camp layout is one authored yard
- Actors pick up light at their feet. Hub squash is one sun mark for the whole yard. Dungeon squash is up to three nearby torches, crystals, or fires, pinned to the current walk frame, faded so marks do not smear
- Dungeon walls are brick ribbons along the floor outline, with caps on tops and ends, not one box per cell
- Floors follow a carved outline: filleted corners, occasional jagged runs, then filled solid. Hall joins keep a closed rim so walkable holes do not open at diagonal bands
- Collision follows that same solid. Ribbons are a thin shell on the outline; you walk the fill, not the brick
- Torches hang on wall brackets and throw a real light disc; crystals and campfires do the same. Occupancy keeps stamps off solid rock
- Ground in the dungeon uses the dungeon-floor sheet; light and shadow paint across the walkable fill
- Interact props register as light sources so benches and pads take the same discs
- Entering a floor still covers immediately; nearby volume streams first. The dungeon scene is packed before the swap
- Returning to the hub no longer freezes the session
- The HUD map well is built when the floor is ready. Opening the map only toggles the overlay; it does not create the picture
- Title load uses the new load background and a dark plate over the art
- Ground scale, wear, outline fillet/jag, and torch / crystal / fire / sun range and energy are on the secret debug menu

Summary: Week 5 Grok Build snapshot. Placeholdia is sheeted and fenced, dungeon walls follow the floor outline with torch light and foot squash, the map is ready on enter, and hub return no longer freezes.
