## 0.5.5

- Dungeon law is maze-first: carve and clean the 1 m grid, then rare angled pieces.
- Floor-wide polyline fillet/jag is no longer the silhouette.
- Stream instances 1 m chunks by default and angled packets as the exception.
- Load-graph gen to volume/buffer edge follows the cleaned grid plus piece hulls.
- Volume skins 1 m maze faces by default.
- Ribbon walls are only for rare angled pieces.
- Buffer occupancy is the cleaned 1 m grid plus angled-piece hulls.
- Light SUB is stamp density, not a floor-wide walk mask.

Summary: Maze-first dungeon law. Angled walls are rare packets, not the floor rim.
