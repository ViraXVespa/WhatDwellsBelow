## 0.3.19

- Puzzle rooms keep every piece on a free cell inside the room
- Small rooms drop hidden chest and crack first, then the visible chest
- A puzzle room gets a pressure plate or a lever, never both
- Stepping on a plate toggles the gate and leaves it open; stepping off does not close it
- Opened chests fade and tint in place and read Empty

Summary: Puzzle rooms shrink to fit, plate and lever are exclusive step toggles, and opened chests stay visually spent.
