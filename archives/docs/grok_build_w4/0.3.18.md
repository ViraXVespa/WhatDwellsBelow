## 0.3.18
- Crystal combat level is walk distance from the entrance only
-- Entrance crystal is always the floor’s first CL (CL 1 on floor 1)
-- Enemy packs still use ±1 CL jitter
- Extra crystals sit on 2-CL walk bands with a 56-cell gap
-- First extra is a find if a band fits; later extras roll a 50% chance, cap 6
- Long dead-end spurs still get a warp; short stubs do not
-- Spur must walk 28 cells to a multi-exit room and stay 32 cells from spawn

Summary: Floor crystals no longer inherit enemy CL jitter or cluster on short spurs; entrance is CL 1 and extras spread across the floor.
